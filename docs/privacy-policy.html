Privacy Policy

Pocket Gaffer · Effective 22 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Pocket Gaffer app for Android ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal or device information. In particular, it has:

  • no user accounts or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind;
  • no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files outside the app.

Information stored on your device

When you play, the app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • the manager name you type in when starting a game;
  • your saved games and app settings.

This information never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, removes this data from your device.

Android backup

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved games in your device backup to your own Google account. This is a feature of Android that you control in your device settings; the developer has no access to these backups.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
